Who is Who?

If you see a piggie who has lots of fluffy and tufty fur, then you are looking at Ancho.

If you are looking at a piggie with much smoother fur and a pointed nose, then that will be Treacle.

Things to Watch For

Pop-corning is when a guinea pig hops straight into the air and twists. It is an involuntary burst of energy usually brought on by sudden joy - especially likely when they find their favourite food, have a fresh clean cage, or are just having fun!

If one of the piggies is lying completely flat, with hind legs stretched out, you have seen them splooting. This is a guinea pig at complete ease and relaxation, feeling very safe in thier environment.

If they both look to suddenly freeze, so that they are statue still, they may have just heard or seen something unfamiliar. Remember, guinea pigs are prey animals and so have a lot of surivival instincts.

You may see one or both of them start running laps around the cage, and jumping over obejcts as they do. This is often called zoomies! This is often pure energy and happiness, and will often be accompanied by pop-corning.
